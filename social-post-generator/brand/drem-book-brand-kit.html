Brand kit · v1.0

Every book starts as a dream.

The logo, colors, type, icons, UI components and social graphics for DREM Book, in one file. Adapted from the structure of the BookBaby template, redrawn as an original identity.

    01 · Logo

    A “D” made of fanned pages

    The mark is a book spine with three pages fanning out to form the letter D, rising like a sun. The wordmark pairs a heavy DREM with a light Book. Every file is vector with text converted to outlines, plus a transparent PNG.

    Using the logo

    Keep it clean and give it room. These rules are drawn from the real mark, not placeholder boxes.

    Clear space

    Leave the width of the spine (x) on every side. Nothing enters this zone: no text, edges or other logos.

    Minimum size

    Below these sizes the fanned pages close up. Use the mark alone when the space is tighter.

    02 · Color

    Midnight ink, ember warmth

    The template’s system, re-hued: a deep ink for text and dark bands, one warm action color with a pale ring, cream paper, and four supporting accents. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

    Proportion

    Roughly 60% Paper and white, 25% Midnight, 10% Ember, 5% everything else.

    Accessible pairings

    Contrast is computed live from the hex values. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.

    03 · Typography

    Open Sans and Zilla Slab

    The same two families the template uses. Open Sans carries interface and headlines; Zilla Slab italic is the editorial accent for emphasis, quotes and book titles. Both are open source (SIL OFL).

    Type scale

    Fluid sizes that shrink smoothly on phones. Copy the CSS from the typography download.

    04 · Icons

    Duotone, drawn on a 48-pixel grid

    Rounded 2.5px strokes with one filled accent, echoing the template’s service icons. Choose a style, then download any icon as SVG or PNG, or take the whole set.

    05 · UI components

    The pieces of the website

    Pill buttons with the pale ring, soft 10px cards, service tiles, steps and the announcement bar: the template’s vocabulary in DREM colors. It is one small stylesheet, prefixed dr-, with no dependencies.

    06 · Social & graphics

    Ready-to-post assets

    Profile images, share cards, cover images and patterns at the exact platform sizes. Change the headline and every graphic redraws.

    Patterns

    Tileable backgrounds built from the fanned-page arc. Use them behind hero bands and print collateral.

    07 · Voice

    Like a friend who has published before

    The template speaks plainly and reassures first-time authors. DREM Book keeps that, with a little more wonder.

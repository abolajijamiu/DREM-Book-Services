    Goals & phases

    Three phases: listen, invite, convert

    The first three months earn trust. The next six grow the audience and start inviting enquiries. The last six add real campaigns with deadlines and offers. Select a month to open it in the calendar.

    Audience

    Who we're talking to

    Independent authors who have a book in them, or already on the page, and need help turning it into a finished book. Four regions, one voice: warm, clear and confident, like a friend who has published before.

    United States & CanadaLargest self-publishing market. Holidays drive Q4. Spelling: follow the author's choice in captions; default to US on LinkedIn and Pinterest.
    United Kingdom & EuropeWorld Book Day in March, Black History Month in October, book fairs in Frankfurt and London.
    Nigeria & West AfricaStrong on Facebook and WhatsApp. Independence Day (Oct 1), Children's Day, Democracy Day, Lagos book festivals.
    WorldwideWorld Book Day (Apr 23), International Literacy Day, Audiobook Month and the November writing challenge unite everyone.

    The first-time novelist

    Has a finished or nearly finished draft and doesn't know what comes next.

    Needs
    Reassurance, a clear path, plain explanations of editing types and costs.
    Best content
    Learn carousels, "what happens next" posts, the Manuscript Readiness Checklist.

    The expert with a nonfiction book

    A coach, pastor, doctor or business owner who wants a book to build authority.

    Needs
    Speed, a professional finish, help outlining from expertise.
    Best content
    LinkedIn posts and blogs, nonfiction outlining guides, timelines.

    The memoir and family-story writer

    Writing their life or family history, often for children and grandchildren.

    Needs
    Warmth, patience, beautiful printed copies to give as gifts.
    Best content
    Memoir prompts, printing and hardcover posts, holiday gift campaigns.

    The children's book author

    Parent, teacher or storyteller with a picture book or early reader.

    Needs
    Illustration and layout guidance, print quality, school and library reach.
    Best content
    Children's Book Day, picture-book blogs, Pinterest pins.

    Content pillars

    Five kinds of post, in a planned mix

    Every post in the calendar belongs to one pillar. Learn leads throughout, because saved and shared tips grow the audience. Offers rise from 11% of posts in phase 1 to 16% in phase 3, and in phase 3 every Learn post also ends with a booking prompt.

    Weekly rhythm

    Four social posts, one blog, every week

    Each post is designed once with the social post generator, then published in every platform size. One of the four posts each week is filmed as a short video when possible.

    MonLearn post

    Carousel, tip or short video. The most saveable post of the week.

    TueBlog goes live

    1,200–1,800 words on the website, shared to LinkedIn and Pinterest.

    WedBlog promo

    One takeaway from the blog, linking to it.

    ThuStories only

    First Thursday of the month: newsletter goes out.

    FriOffer or behind the book

    Services, the process, client stories, seasonal offers.

    SatRest

    Reply to comments and messages.

    SunCommunity or Dream

    Questions, polls, quotes, win threads.

    Key dates add a light story post on the day (Instagram and Facebook Stories, WhatsApp Status). Stories on other days are optional: reshare the week's posts, polls, and behind-the-scenes clips.

    Blog & newsletter

    The blog answers what authors search for

    Each of the 66 posts targets one search phrase an author would type, like "types of book editing" or "what is an ISBN". Social posts bring people to the blog, and the blog turns readers into newsletter subscribers and enquiries.

    Every blog post

    1. Put the search phrase in the title, first paragraph and one subheadingWrite for a person first. The keyword is in the calendar's Search keyword column.
    2. Answer the question in the first 100 wordsThen go deeper with steps, examples and a checklist.
    3. Link to two older posts and one service pageBuild a web of helpful pages over the 15 months.
    4. End with the phase's call to actionPhase 1: free checklist. Phase 2: discovery call. Phase 3: the season's deadline.
    5. Make a Pinterest pin and a Wednesday promo from itThe generator makes both from one entry.

    Monthly newsletter

    First Thursday of every month, 15 issues. The sign-up gift is the free Manuscript Readiness Checklist, promoted in phase 1 CTAs.

    • A short note from the founderWhat's happening at DREM Book this month, in first person.
    • The month's best blog postPlus one quick tip not posted anywhere else.
    • One author story or reader questionReal people only, with permission.
    • One clear next stepThe month's campaign or offer, never more than one.

    Measure & review

    What to track, and when to change course

    Record baselines in the first six weeks, then set number targets from real results instead of guesses. Keep one simple monthly sheet: followers, reach, saves, link clicks, newsletter subscribers, enquiries, and where each enquiry came from.

    Numbers that matter

    SignalTells you
    Saves & sharesThe content is useful enough to keep (the best sign on Instagram and LinkedIn)
    Newsletter sign-upsAudience you own, whatever the platforms do
    Blog visits from searchThe blog is working long term (Google Search Console)
    Messages & enquiriesPeople are ready to talk about their book
    Booked projects by sourceWhich platform and post actually brings clients

    Review rhythm

    1. Weekly, 15 minutesReply to every comment and message. Note questions authors ask: they become posts.
    2. Monthly, 1 hourFill the tracking sheet. Repeat the top three posts' formats next month; drop what flopped twice.
    3. End of each phaseCompare with the phase goal. Adjust the mix, platforms and offers for the next phase.
    4. December 2027Use 15 months of results to write the 2028 plan.

    Workflow

    Make a month's content in one sitting

    Batch the work: plan and design next month's posts in the last week of each month, then schedule them all at once.

    1. Week 3: writeDraft next month's 4 blogs and captions from the calendar topics.
    2. Week 4: designAdd the posts to content/posts.json and run the generator for every platform size.
    3. Week 4: filmRecord the month's short videos in one session.
    4. Week 4: scheduleLoad everything into Meta Business Suite (Instagram, Facebook), Buffer or Later (the rest), and the blog and email tools.
    5. Every day: show up15 minutes replying, plus stories when there's something to share.
    Real people, with permission

    Client stories, photos and quotes only with written consent. Never invent testimonials, reviews or numbers.

    Say it plainly

    "Your book", not "the product". "As fast as 3 days" only if true. No hype.

    Brand kit on everything

    The generator applies the logo, colors and fonts. Keep one offer per week at most.

    Accessible posts

    Alt text on every image, captions on every video, capitalise each word in hashtags (#SelfPublishing) so screen readers read them correctly.

    Quotes checked

    Only use quotes with a known source. When in doubt, attribute it as a proverb or leave it out.

    Stay flexible

    The calendar is a plan, not a contract. Swap in timely news, client wins and questions from comments.
